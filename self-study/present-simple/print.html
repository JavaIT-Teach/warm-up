1. He works, she works

For things that are always true and habits (every day, on Mondays, usually).

I / you / we / theywork
he / she / itworks
watch, wash, go, do→ watches, washes, goes, does
study, cry→ studies, cries
have→ has

2. Negative: don't / doesn't

I / you / we / they don't + verb
he / she / it doesn't + verb
She doesn't work. ✔
She doesn't works. ✘  (no -s after doesn't)

3. Questions: Do…? / Does…?

Do / Does + person + verb …?
Do you like tea?
— Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
Does she like tea?
— Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.

Do → I / you / we / they. Does → he / she / it. After Does: no -s (Does he lives?).

4. Question words

What?a thingWhat do you eat? — Rice.
Where?a placeWhere do you work? — In a bank.
When?a time / dayWhen do you study? — On Tuesdays.
What time?clock timeWhat time do you get up? — At 7.
Who?a personWho do you live with? — My sister.
Why?a reasonWhy do you learn English? — Because…
How?the wayHow do you go to work? — By bus.
How many?a numberHow many children do you have? — Two.
How old?ageHow old is your son? — He's 8.

5. Wh- questions: word order

Question word + do / does + person + verb …?

Where do you live?  ·  What does your sister do?  ·  What time do they have lunch?

Same as a Yes/No question — just put the question word at the start: Do you live here? → Where do you live?
